\section{Thesis Outline}
\label{sec:thesis-outline}

This thesis is organized into seven chapters as follows.

\textbf{Chapter 1 -- Introduction} presents the motivation for developing Stockrium and the problem addressed by the thesis. It defines the project's primary objectives, application scope, and overall thesis structure.

\textbf{Chapter 2 -- Related Work and Foundation Technologies} reviews studies on financial decision support, large language models, multi-agent systems, news sentiment, and Vietnamese stock-market prediction. It then introduces the financial, agentic AI, backtesting, and development technologies required to understand Stockrium.

\textbf{Chapter 3 -- Proposed Application} describes the application idea, target stakeholders, principal use cases, and user flow. It also presents the key mobile, analytical, conversational, administrative, and backtesting capabilities of the proposed system.

\textbf{Chapter 4 -- System Design and Implementation} explains the overall architecture and the design of the mobile application, backend services, storage, and data-acquisition pipelines. It also details the agentic stock-analysis workflow and the integration of its specialized analytical components.

\textbf{Chapter 5 -- System Testing} defines the testing objectives, environment, and verification strategy for the implemented system. It reports functional and integration testing of the application, backend services, authentication mechanisms, and AI workflows.

\textbf{Chapter 6 -- Experiments and Evaluation} defines the evaluation questions and evidence, evaluates the analytical pipeline on historical VN30 data, compares it with explicit baselines, assesses robustness and statistical significance, and discusses threats to validity.

\textbf{Chapter 7 -- Conclusion and Future Work} summarizes the research outcomes, principal contributions, and degree to which the stated objectives were achieved. It discusses the remaining limitations and proposes directions for extending the data, analytical methods, system, and evaluation.
