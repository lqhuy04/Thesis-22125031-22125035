\section{Application Scope}
\label{sec:application-scope}

\subsection{Target Users and Interfaces}

Stockrium primarily serves Vietnamese retail investors. The mobile application serves beginners seeking accessible financial information as well as experienced individual investors interested in configuring analytical inputs and examining the resulting assessments. The internal experimentation and backtesting web interface is intended only for authorized evaluators, who run analysis over selected stock groups, execute controlled backtests, compare supported configurations, and review stored results; it is not a public investment interface. Keeping these capabilities in a separate interface isolates privileged, resource-intensive, and experiment-oriented operations from routine investor use. Backtest results allow evaluators to identify weaknesses and refine the default weights, analytical selections, and decision rules used by the automatic analysis flow, which is intended to be the primary path for most users. Any resulting changes must be validated on separate historical periods to reduce overfitting and should not be interpreted as guaranteed improvements in future investment performance.

\subsection{Functional Scope}

The mobile application is available exclusively on the Android platform. Its functional scope includes authentication, market exploration, stock search, company information, price charts, technical indicators, fundamental data, financial news, a personal watchlist, and natural-language interaction. Its intelligent-analysis function combines technical, fundamental, and news evidence through specialized analytical nodes in a multi-agent workflow to generate an explainable recommendation and confidence score. For an accepted \textit{Buy} recommendation, the result may additionally include entry, take-profit, stop-loss, and holding-period information. Users may also configure selected analytical components and their relative weights. Backtesting is restricted to the internal experimentation and backtesting web interface and evaluates the Stockrium pipeline rather than arbitrary user-defined trading strategies. Stockrium is limited to decision support: it does not place, modify, or cancel buy or sell orders; connect to brokerage firms or other financial institutions; transfer funds; automatically execute recommendations; or perform portfolio construction, optimization, and rebalancing. It is not intended for high-frequency trading or professional financial advisory services.

\subsection{Market and Data Scope}

The data scope is limited in Vietnam, data-acquisition pipelines are functionally capable of covering the entire Vietnamese equity market including stocks listed on HOSE, HNX, and UPCoM and representative domestic market indices; however, current storage capacity restricts coverage to the 100 securities in the VN100 index, which represent the largest and most liquid segment of the market. The system processes current and historical prices, trading volumes, company profiles, financial statements and ratios, technical indicators, and Vietnamese financial news, subject to the availability and quality of the integrated sources. Foreign equities, bonds, commodities, foreign exchange, cryptocurrencies, and derivatives are outside the scope of this thesis.
\subsection{Evaluation Scope}

The system is evaluated using historical Vietnamese market data to examine the behavior, explainability, risk controls, and performance of the analytical pipeline. Backtesting reports measures such as trade count, win rate, total return, Sharpe ratio, and maximum drawdown. Because the evaluation is a simplified historical simulation, it does not reproduce every condition of live execution, including order-book depth, latency, and partial fills. Its results are used to assess the implemented approach and do not guarantee future investment performance.
