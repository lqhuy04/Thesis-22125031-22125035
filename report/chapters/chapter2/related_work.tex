\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related-work}

Research related to Stockrium lies at the intersection of financial decision support, large language models, multi-agent systems, financial-news sentiment analysis, and commercial brokerage platforms in Vietnam. This section reviews representative studies and platforms in these areas and identifies the gap addressed by the proposed application.

\subsection{LLM-Based Agent Systems for Financial Decision Support}

LLM-based agent systems organize financial reasoning through specialized roles, memory structures, or both. TradingAgents is a representative multi-agent framework that models a trading firm through analyst agents, Bull and Bear researchers, a trader, and a risk-management team \cite{xiao2024tradingagents}. The authors report that this structured collaboration combines heterogeneous evidence and improves selected return and risk measures. Its multi-round debates nevertheless require repeated model calls and can therefore increase latency and inference cost, while the final action remains strongly dependent on the language model's reasoning path. FinMem instead equips a single trading agent with layered memory that retrieves financial information according to temporal relevance \cite{yu2023finmem}, highlighting the value of maintaining context across changing market conditions, although its main objective remains autonomous trading performance. Stockrium adopts role specialization within a bounded multi-agent graph designed for Vietnamese equities: technical, fundamental, and news analytical agents operate in parallel, after which their evidence is synthesized. Deterministic scoring and validation are added around the LLM output to improve traceability and constrain the final recommendation. Thus, Stockrium is positioned as a user-facing decision-support system rather than an autonomous trading agent or a debate-based simulation of a financial institution.

\subsection{Finance-Specific Large Language Models}

Finance-specific language models show that domain data and adaptation can improve the interpretation of specialized terminology, disclosures, and market communication. BloombergGPT is a 50-billion-parameter BLOOM-style model trained from scratch on a mixed corpus comprising extensive financial data and general-purpose text; its evaluation indicates that this domain-focused pretraining improves performance on financial natural-language-processing tasks while retaining general capabilities \cite{wu2023bloomberggpt}. Stockrium's current implementation instead uses general-purpose hosted models within a controlled application workflow, where structured data processing and deterministic rules remain responsible for calculations and output validation while language-model nodes interpret and synthesize prepared evidence. As sufficient Vietnamese market and news data become available, evaluating a finance-specific model or an adapted foundation model for the same analytical roles is a viable future direction.

\subsection{Financial News Sentiment Analysis}

Financial news may contain events and expectations that are not fully reflected in historical prices, but its specialized vocabulary limits the effectiveness of general sentiment models. FinBERT addresses this problem through domain-specific pretraining and fine-tuning, achieving improved positive, neutral, and negative classification on financial datasets \cite{araci2019finbert}. FinBERT-LSTM subsequently combined news sentiment with historical prices for NASDAQ-100 prediction, showing that textual information can supplement numerical time series \cite{halder2022finbertlstm}; however, it remains a price-prediction pipeline and does not reconcile news with fundamental and technical evidence. Vietnamese studies provide more locally relevant findings. Tung et al. reported that PhoBERT outperformed conventional models on Vietnamese stock-article titles, reaching approximately 93\% accuracy \cite{tung2021phobert}. Using a year-long collection from three Vietnamese financial-news platforms, Vu et al. achieved over 81\% accuracy in binary positive--negative sentiment classification \cite{vu2023sentiments}. Their event study found significant changes in the return variance of the VN30 and HNX30 indices around negative news over the examined 30-day windows, but no statistically significant changes in mean returns. These results suggest that sentiment can provide useful contextual and risk information but should not independently determine an investment action. Stockrium therefore summarizes and assesses stock-related news as one analytical source whose output is combined with technical and fundamental evidence.

\subsection{Commercial Platforms}

SSI iBoard Pro is a comprehensive brokerage application that supports market monitoring, watchlists, portfolio management, and securities order execution. Its iResearch area provides analyst-curated recommendations, company and market reports, suggested buy ranges, target and stop-loss levels, and recommendation-performance tracking; the platform also offers paper trading with live market data \cite{ssiIboardResearch2026,ssiIboardLaunch2023}. Since June 2026, the application has additionally integrated the Cu DOHA AI assistant, which answers investment questions and combines fundamental and technical perspectives in stock-level analysis \cite{ssiCuDoha2026}.

The CVS securities service on MoMo focuses on making stock-market access simple for newer and small-scale investors. Users can open a CVS account, buy and sell stocks, and deposit or withdraw money directly within MoMo using a CVS balance, MoMo, the Tui Than Tai service, or a linked bank. The service permits purchases from one share and approximately VND~10,000 and automatically separates board-lot and odd-lot orders \cite{momoCVSSecurities2026}.

Stockrium differs from these brokerage applications through a configurable and inspectable analytical workflow rather than transaction execution. It separately analyzes technical, fundamental, and Vietnamese-news evidence, exposes a score and explanation for each component, supports manual source selection and weighting or automatic investment-horizon-based configuration, and applies deterministic Buy-or-Wait and trading-plan validation rules. It can also backtest the same analytical pipeline against engine-only, buy-and-hold, and single-indicator alternatives; these capabilities are not described in the reviewed official documentation for SSI iBoard Pro or CVS on MoMo, while Stockrium itself does not open brokerage accounts, hold assets, or place market orders.

\subsection{Research Gap and Positioning of Stockrium}

The reviewed research and commercial platforms reveal a separation among key capabilities: sentiment studies classify news, finance-specific LLMs address language understanding, multi-agent frameworks often target autonomous trading in international markets, and brokerage applications prioritize analysis services and transaction execution within their own ecosystems. SSI iBoard Pro demonstrates that AI-assisted fundamental and technical analysis is already available in a Vietnamese brokerage application, while CVS on MoMo demonstrates how securities access can be simplified for small-scale and newer investors. The narrower gap addressed by Stockrium is the combination of technical, fundamental, and Vietnamese-news evidence in a user-facing application with configurable source selection and weighting, investment-horizon-aware planning, explicit component scores, deterministic decision constraints, and internal backtesting of the supported analytical pipeline. Stockrium's explicit multi-agent workflow coordinates specialized analytical agents and uses language models to interpret and synthesize prepared evidence rather than perform every calculation. Deterministic components calculate indicators and weighted scores, enforce the Buy-or-Wait thresholds, and validate trading-plan constraints; the aggregator subsequently generates a bounded confidence assessment and explanation. The internal backtesting facility enables the pipeline and its supported configurations to be evaluated on historical Vietnamese data. The contribution is therefore not a new forecasting model, a brokerage service, or a claim of guaranteed superior returns; it is the construction of a localized, configurable, and transparent decision-support environment that brings these analytical capabilities into one inspectable system.
