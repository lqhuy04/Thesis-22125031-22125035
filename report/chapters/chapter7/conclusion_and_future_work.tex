\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:chapter7-conclusion}

This thesis presented Stockrium, an integrated multi-agent decision-support and research platform for the Vietnamese equity market. The work was motivated by a practical information problem: an individual investor must often combine price behavior, company fundamentals, and financial news while navigating data from separate sources and interpreting results without a consistent analytical framework. Stockrium addresses this problem through a mobile application, a layered backend, a coordinated multi-agent workflow, structured data-acquisition pipelines, and a historical backtesting subsystem. The system is designed to organize evidence and explain an assessment; it does not replace investor judgment or execute trades.

The implemented architecture separates presentation, domain services, data storage, deterministic financial computation, and language-model reasoning. Within the multi-agent workflow, technical, fundamental, and article agents examine different evidence domains. The recommendation stage deterministically applies weighted component scores and technical acceptance thresholds, while the aggregator generates a bounded confidence value and structured explanation without changing the established Buy-or-Wait decision. This design makes individual evidence contributions visible, allows analytical branches to be enabled or disabled, and supports reuse of the same logic in interactive analysis and historical evaluation.

The historical experiment provides a deliberately cautious view of the analytical results. On the current 30-security VN30 stock set, evaluated from August~1, 2024 to August~1, 2026, the complete pipeline executed 440 trades compared with 578 for the engine-only branch. It had a smaller mean maximum drawdown and modestly higher median return, geometric aggregate return, mean and median win rates, and median trade-level Sharpe statistic, while mean return was slightly lower. It outperformed the engine-only result for 16 of 30 securities and buy-and-hold for only 3 of 30. Because the full branch changes both candidate acceptance and trade management, the differences cannot be attributed to filtering alone. Statistical and robustness evidence remained limited, and the results do not establish reliable market outperformance or guaranteed future profit.

The first objective was achieved at the prototype level. Stockrium provides a coherent path from market discovery to evidence inspection and AI-assisted synthesis. Its configurable analysis supports both a simplified automatic mode and a manual mode in which analytical perspectives and weights can be selected. The structured response exposes component scores, confidence, explanations, and risk-related price levels when a Buy recommendation is accepted. These properties make the result more inspectable than a single unrestricted language-model response. Nevertheless, completion of the software prototype is not equivalent to proving that every recommendation is correct or that the application is ready for unrestricted financial use.

The second objective was achieved as an engineering and evaluation capability. The backtesting subsystem reuses the production multi-agent logic, indicator service, and deterministic technical-scoring module, prevents same-close execution, includes a warm-up interval, applies an explicit fixed transaction cost, and records full-pipeline and engine-only results. Sharing the implementation removes the drift risk created by separately maintained agent-level and historical scoring rules. More importantly, the evaluation reports unfavorable and non-significant outcomes rather than selecting only positive cases. This makes the framework useful for diagnosing behavior even though the current evidence does not establish profitable performance.

The third objective remains incomplete. Stockrium establishes a common schema and maintainable pipelines for the three evidence domains, which is a necessary foundation for a Vietnamese-market research dataset. However, public research use requires more than populated application tables. Each release must define its observation interval, source and licensing conditions, schema version, missing-data policy, collection time, correction history, and information-availability time. Until those controls and a versioned release are completed, the data should be described as a curated application dataset rather than a finished public benchmark.

\section{Future Work}
\label{sec:future-work}

\subsection{Backtesting System Improvements}

The backtesting system should first improve the integrity of historical inputs. Fundamental records need filing-availability timestamps, and simulations should use only information that was publicly available on each decision date. Historical index membership should be reconstructed by effective date to reduce survivorship bias, while news records should retain publication, retrieval, source, and correction metadata. Versioned data snapshots would then provide a stable basis for comparing experiments.

Reproducibility should be strengthened by storing an immutable experiment manifest containing the security universe, evaluation interval, parameters, code revision, model and prompt versions, data snapshot, transaction-cost assumptions, and random seeds. Caching model outputs would support deterministic replay, while longer out-of-sample periods and historically reconstructed universes would provide more representative bull, sideways, and bear-market samples.

The simulator should also move from independent per-security calculations toward a portfolio-level execution model with shared capital, position sizing, cash and concurrent-exposure constraints, and portfolio risk measures. Gap-aware fills, Vietnamese price-limit rules, liquidity filters, spread and slippage estimates, and intraday data where available would make execution more realistic. Recording forward outcomes for rejected candidates would additionally support confidence calibration and a less selection-biased assessment of the Buy gate.

\subsection{Analytical and Multi-Agent Improvements}

Structured outputs do not eliminate reasoning or classification errors in generated recommendations and article labels, and the current confidence value has not been calibrated against observed outcomes. Human review therefore remains necessary. Future versions should strengthen evidence grounding by requiring each specialist output to reference the exact candles, financial periods, ratios, and articles used in its reasoning. The aggregator can then verify evidence availability and reject unsupported claims before generating a recommendation. Confidence should be calibrated against observed forward outcomes rather than interpreted only as a weighted internal score.

Additional research can compare the current analytical-node decomposition with deterministic ranking models, classical machine-learning methods, time-series models, and Vietnamese finance-specific language models under the same point-in-time protocol. Model and prompt ablations should identify whether observed changes arise from new information, the specialist decomposition, or language-model variability. Human expert review can be incorporated to assess factual grounding, explanation quality, usefulness, and disagreement between analysts and automated outputs.

\subsection{System Quality and User Experience}

The platform depends on external market-data, search, storage, and language-model services. Changes in their availability, response formats, quotas, or model behavior can affect system reliability and analytical reproducibility. The current prototype also requires broader security, load, recovery, Android-device compatibility, accessibility, and longitudinal usability validation before production-scale deployment.

Future development should therefore extend automated unit, integration, contract, end-to-end, security, and performance coverage and execute it in continuous integration. Long-running collection, analysis, and backtesting jobs should use durable background queues with progress reporting, retry policies, idempotency keys, and operational monitoring. Secrets management, audit logging, rate limiting, dependency scanning, backup restoration, and incident procedures should be strengthened for a production deployment.

The mobile application should also subscribe to and handle WebSocket events directly so that market data, analysis status, and other relevant screen state can be updated as events arrive instead of requiring users to refresh manually. The implementation should include reconnection, resubscription, heartbeat handling, and stale-data indicators to keep real-time updates reliable and improve the user experience when network conditions change.

User-facing research should examine whether investors correctly understand confidence values, explanations, risk levels, and the distinction between decision support and advice. Accessibility, localization quality, onboarding, notification controls, and explanation design can be evaluated with representative Vietnamese retail investors. Portfolio tracking and paper-trading features could support learning and retrospective review without enabling automatic execution. Any later brokerage integration would require separate legal, security, suitability, and operational analysis beyond the scope of the present thesis.

\section{Closing Remarks}
\label{sec:closing-remarks}

Stockrium demonstrates that heterogeneous Vietnamese market evidence can be organized into a coherent mobile and research platform and processed through an inspectable combination of deterministic analytics and specialized language-model reasoning nodes. Its strongest result is not a claim of superior returns, but the construction of an end-to-end environment in which analytical assumptions, decisions, baselines, and limitations can be examined together.

The evaluation also shows why caution is essential in financial AI. A system may generate structured explanations and reduce exposure while still failing to outperform simple alternatives consistently. By reporting this distinction and identifying the data and experimental controls still required, the thesis establishes a practical foundation for future work on more reproducible, realistic, and trustworthy decision support for the Vietnamese stock market.
