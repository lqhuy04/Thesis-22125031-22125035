\chapter*{ABSTRACT}
\phantomsection
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{ABSTRACT}

Vietnamese retail investors must often combine fragmented market prices, company fundamentals, technical indicators, and financial news before making an informed investment decision. This process requires both financial knowledge and substantial time, while existing tools commonly present only one analytical perspective or provide recommendations whose supporting evidence is difficult to inspect. This thesis presents \textit{Stockrium}, an integrated Vietnamese equity-market decision-support and research platform designed to organize heterogeneous financial information and produce explainable, user-configurable analysis.

Stockrium consists of an Android mobile application, a backend server, an internal experimentation and backtesting web interface, data-acquisition pipelines, and a multi-agent workflow. The platform supports market exploration, stock search, interactive price and technical-indicator charts, company and fundamental information, financial news, watchlist, AI-assisted analysis based on investment-horizon preference, and an independent conversational financial assistant. Given a user's investment horizon, independent technical, fundamental, and news agents retrieve and analyze their respective evidence, each producing a normalized score and a natural-language summary. These scores are combined through horizon-dependent weights into a Buy/Wait recommendation, accompanied, for Buy decisions, by a trading plan and a confidence-scored explanation of the overall decision. Separate crawling and scheduled collection processes acquire and normalize market, company, financial-statement, and news data for application use and historical evaluation.

System testing covered 62 backend API operations, the complete multi-agent analysis and backtesting flows, and manual walkthroughs of 10 mobile and 8 experimentation-web workflow groups; all reported tests passed. Professional feedback from Ms.~Trinh, a securities analyst at FPT Securities Joint Stock Company, indicated that the application presents comprehensive stock information through an accessible interface and that its AI-generated analysis is clear and useful as a starting point for further review. A historical evaluation on the 30 VN30 securities from August~1, 2024 to August~1, 2026, against engine-only, buy-and-hold, and single-indicator baselines, showed that the complete pipeline executed 23.9\% fewer trades and had a smaller mean maximum drawdown (23.82\%~$\rightarrow$~21.64\%), with modest gains in median return. Mean return was slightly lower, and the pipeline outperformed buy-and-hold on only 3 of 30 securities. Statistical and robustness evidence remained limited. Therefore, Stockrium's principal contribution is an integrated and inspectable environment for Vietnamese-market decision support and experimentation, rather than evidence of guaranteed profitability or a replacement for professional judgment.

\textbf{Keywords:} Vietnamese stock market, decision support, multi-agent systems, large language models, financial data analysis, backtesting.
